# Phase 3: Open Questions

## Purpose and boundary

Catalogue unresolved questions that could affect the project. The purpose is to
understand alternatives and determine what needs a construction assumption, not
to force every mystery into an answer.

## Required outputs

Create `questions/index.md` and one file per substantial question using:

```markdown
# Question

## Evidence
## Possible interpretations
## Arguments and counterarguments
## Confidence
## Required decision or preservation boundary
```

Mark whether Phase 4 must resolve a bounded practical question or whether the
mystery should remain protected.

## Completion check

The index distinguishes decisions necessary for construction from questions the
story may honestly preserve.
