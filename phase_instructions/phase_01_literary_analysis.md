# Phase 1: Literary Analysis

## Purpose and boundary

Analyze the permitted source corpus, or establish the baseline for an original
work, before constructing the story. Record evidence, cross-text development,
and unresolved questions. Do not approve plot, future history, identities, or
solutions in this phase.

Keep source fact, synthesis, speculation, and unknowns visibly separate. Use
edition-and-location locators. Do not record remembered wording as a quotation.

## Required outputs

Create `analysis/index.md` and the documents relevant to this project:

- `themes.md` — recurring themes, tensions, and changes;
- `philosophy.md` — core ideas, their evidence, development, and open questions;
- `style.md` — descriptive analysis of organization, dialogue, pacing,
  exposition, interiority, action, ambiguity, and what the source avoids;
- `voice.md` — analysis of rhythm, density, dialogue, and information flow;
- `timeline.md` — chronology, with uncertainty marked;
- `world_state.md` — relevant political, social, ecological, technical,
  religious, and economic conditions at the chosen starting point.

For an original work, replace source analysis with a clear creative baseline and
research only the real-world material the project needs.

## Completion check

Claims are traceable, gaps remain visible, and style analysis has not become a
licence to copy an identifiable author's expression.
