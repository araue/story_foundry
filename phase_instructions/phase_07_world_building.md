# Phase 7: Required World Building and Maps

## Purpose and boundary

Build only material required by approved assumptions, architecture, and arcs.
Create `world/index.md` and separate established facts, approved construction,
disputed interpretation, and deliberately open detail. Institutions need
material needs, internal pressures, and limits; capabilities need history, cost,
and consequences.

## Reader-facing readiness

For each consequential world element, record its immediate function, material
encounter, ordinary participant knowledge, minimum context needed before first
use, and what remains deferred. An on-page practice or institution is incomplete
when it is merely a label: define what people do, where and with whom, its
practical purpose, what it costs or risks, and what can go wrong.

World files are author knowledge. Phase 5 plans their introduction; Phase 8 must
make it understandable in the manuscript.

## Geography and maps

Create maps only when geography affects causation. Maintain `world/places.md` as
the location register. New routes, distances, orientations, boundaries, or
infrastructure links require an approved assumption before they govern prose.

Store an editable schematic continuity map in `world/maps/`; derive any
reader-facing image only from approved locations. Do not invent details to fill
blank space. Before drafting, check locations, movement, direction, sight lines,
and travel intervals against the register and map.

An optional `epub/map.md` may embed one reader-facing image after the table of
contents. Its map must have meaningful alternative text and may not introduce
new story facts.

## Completion check

Required places, dependencies, institutions, ecology, and technology are
coherent and each element needed by the next chapter has observable action,
plain purpose, participant perspective, and present cost or consequence.
